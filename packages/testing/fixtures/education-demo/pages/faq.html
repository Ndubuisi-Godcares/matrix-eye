Frequently Asked Questions

Can I transfer credits from another university?

Yes. You may transfer up to 40% of the credits required for your degree, after the faculty evaluates your transcripts.

Are there part-time programmes?

Undergraduate programmes are full-time only. The only part-time option is the evening MBA, which runs on weekday evenings.

Can I defer my admission?

Yes. Admitted students may defer their place for one year by writing to the admissions office before the semester begins.

Is there support for students with disabilities?

The disability support office arranges accessible rooms, exam accommodations and assistive technology. Please contact the office early.
