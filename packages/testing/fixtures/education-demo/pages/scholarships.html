Scholarships and Bursaries

Merit scholarship

The merit scholarship covers 50% of tuition for students who enter with a GPA above 3.8 and is renewed each year while the student keeps a GPA above 3.5.

Need-based bursary

The need-based bursary provides up to $1,500 per year for students from low-income families. Applicants must submit proof of household income. The deadline to apply for a bursary is 15 July.

Sports scholarship

Talented athletes in football, basketball and athletics may receive a partial sports scholarship after trials held every August.
