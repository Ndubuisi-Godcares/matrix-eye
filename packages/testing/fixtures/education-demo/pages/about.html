About Riverside University

Our history

Riverside University was founded in 1962 as a teacher training college and received full university status in 1988. Today the university has about 12,000 students and more than 600 academic staff.

Mission

Our mission is to educate practical, ethical graduates who solve real problems in their communities. We combine rigorous teaching with work placements and applied research.

Accreditation

All programmes are accredited by the national higher education commission. The nursing programme is additionally accredited by the National Nursing Council.
