BSc Computer Science

Overview

The four year BSc Computer Science programme prepares graduates for software engineering, data science and cybersecurity careers. Students build a portfolio of real projects from the first year.

Modules

Core modules include programming, data structures and algorithms, databases, operating systems, computer networks, software engineering and machine learning. In the final year students complete a capstone project with an industry partner.

Entry requirements

Five credits including English, Mathematics and Physics or Further Mathematics.
