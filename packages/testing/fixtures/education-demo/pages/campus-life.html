Campus Life

Clubs and societies

Students run more than 40 clubs, including debate, robotics, drama, entrepreneurship and a choir. New students can join at the clubs fair in the first week of each semester.

Sports

The university has football, basketball and athletics teams, plus a gym open to all students. Intramural leagues run every semester.

Library

The library is open Monday to Friday from 8am to 10pm, and on Saturday from 9am to 5pm. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Students can borrow up to ten books for two weeks.

Health and wellbeing

A campus clinic offers free consultations to students, and a counselling service runs weekday appointments.
