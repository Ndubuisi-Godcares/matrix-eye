Other fees

A one-time registration fee of $120 is charged when you first enrol. Students also pay a yearly library and technology fee of $85.

Payment options

Tuition can be paid in two instalments per year, one at the start of each semester. Payments are accepted by bank transfer or online card payment. Scholarships are deducted from the first instalment.
