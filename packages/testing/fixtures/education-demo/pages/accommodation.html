Accommodation

On-campus hostels

Riverside has 1,200 beds in on-campus hostels. A single room costs $900 per semester and a shared room costs $600 per semester. Rooms are allocated after you accept your offer, in order of payment.

Off-campus housing

The housing office keeps a list of approved private landlords near campus. Typical rents are listed on the housing portal, and the office can help with contracts.

Facilities

All hostels have reliable internet, laundry rooms and shared kitchens. Hostels are within a ten minute walk of lecture halls.
