Admissions

Entry requirements for undergraduate programmes

Applicants need a minimum of five credits at secondary school level, including English Language and Mathematics. Computer Science and Civil Engineering also require a credit in Physics or Further Mathematics. Nursing requires a credit in Biology.

Application deadlines

The application deadline for the September intake is 30 June 2026. The deadline for the January intake is 30 November 2026. Late applications are considered only if places remain.

Application fee

There is a non-refundable application fee of $50, payable online when you submit your application.

International students

International applicants whose first language is not English must provide an IELTS score of at least 6.0, or an equivalent test result. International students also need a valid student visa before arriving on campus.

How to apply

Create an account on the applications portal, upload your certificates and personal statement, and pay the application fee. You will receive a decision by email within four weeks.
