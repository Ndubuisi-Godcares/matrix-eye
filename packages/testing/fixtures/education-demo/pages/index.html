Welcome to Riverside University

Riverside University is a public university with about 12,000 students across three faculties: Science and Technology, Business and Management, and Health Sciences. We offer undergraduate degrees designed with employers and a campus built around student life.

Why study at Riverside

  • Small classes with a 15 to 1 student to lecturer ratio.
  • Paid internships in the final year for every programme.
  • Scholarships and bursaries for talented and hard-working students.
